# Google Stitch Prompt

You are the lead Product Designer, UX Architect, and Design Systems Engineer for this project.

## Phase 1 — Deep Product Understanding

Study **every `.md` file** in this project thoroughly before generating or modifying any UI.

Do not skim.

For each document:

- Understand the business requirements.
- Understand the insurance workflows.
- Understand every actor involved.
- Understand business rules.
- Understand permissions.
- Understand user journeys.
- Understand workflows between modules.
- Understand dependencies.
- Understand navigation relationships.
- Understand data relationships.
- Understand edge cases.
- Understand states (loading, empty, error, success).
- Understand actions available to every user.

Build a complete internal mental model of the software.
